# When stuck — the calm list

Nothing here is your fault, and nothing here loses your work. Pick the line that matches.

**The preview is blank / doesn't load.**
Type `/fix` and say "blank page". Claude checks the preview server and restarts it.

**There's an error message in the chat.**
Don't read it. Type `/fix`. If Claude asks what you were doing, answer in one line.

**It built something that doesn't look like my Figma.**
Say what's different in your words: "the heading is too small and breaks into three lines", "the cards should be in one row". Claude fixes and re-shoots. If you're unsure, ask: "show me the screenshots next to my frame."

**It changed something I didn't ask for.**
Say "you changed X — put it back." If it's bigger than one thing: `/undo` and pick the last good save point.

**Motion feels wrong.**
Use these words: *too fast*, *too much*, *too bouncy*, *should hold longer*, *should be subtler*. Or: "remove the effect on the cards."

**Claude asks me a technical question I don't understand.**
Say: "I don't know — choose the most standard option and tell me in one line." That is always allowed.

**Claude asked me to log in somewhere (Figma, GitHub, Vercel).**
That's normal the first time. A browser window opens; log in with your own account; come back and say "done".

**Claude is doing something for a long time.**
Installing, building and screenshots take up to a minute. If it's more than three minutes with no message, say "status?".

**I closed the app / it crashed / my laptop died.**
Open the folder again and type `/help`. Everything is saved in `brief/` and in save points.

**I want to go back to yesterday's version.**
`/undo list` → choose.

**I want to start over.**
Copy the kit folder again. The old project stays where it is.

**The client wants changes after it's live.**
Tell Claude what they said, in their words. Then `/ship` for a preview link, `/ship prod` when approved.

**Nothing works and I'm out of patience.**
Type `/fix` and say "I'm stuck, please save everything and tell me where we are." Then take a break. It'll be there.
