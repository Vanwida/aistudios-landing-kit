What to type, and what happens

Keep this open while you work. Find the line, copy the phrase, paste it into the chat with Claude. When Claude asks a question with options, click one.

Starting, or coming back

  1. 1Open the Claude app → Code → open your project folder.
  2. 2Set the permission mode to Auto (the selector at the bottom of the chat).
  3. 3/helpClaude says where the project is and what to say next./startNew project — then choose “New landing page from a brief”.

The work, in order

You want to…Type thisWhat happens
Start a new project
/startthen choose “New landing page from a brief”
 Claude asks about the client, then the site — sections, navigation, hero, motion. Click the options. Then the preview and the board open in your browser.
Use a site you love as the reference
https://www.lassie.ai/paste any address, and say what you like about it
 Claude studies its layout, type, colour and motion. The site questions come pre-filled from it.
Get a first design in Figma
design it
 Claude drafts the site in Figma — frames, colours, type, real copy. Open Figma. It's a draft.
Make the design yours Nothing to type — work in Figma. Refine, don't rebuild. When you're happy, export each section frame as a PNG into the folder brief/figma/ in your project.
Bring your Figma into the kit
import
 Colours, fonts, spacing and the list of sections are read from your Figma and PNGs. Check them on the board.
Build the next section
next
 One section is built. On the board: screenshots next to your frame — desktop, tablet, phone. Look.

Check everything before sending

 Phone, tablet, accessibility, speed, links. Claude fixes what it can and asks the rest with options.

 You get a preview link. First time: the browser asks you to log in to Vercel — use your own account — then type done.

 The page goes live. Claude handles everything underneath.

Go back to how it was before

 Back to the last save point. Nothing is ever lost — the save points are on the board.
Get the latest version of the kit
update the kit
 Claude fetches AISTUDIOS's current version of the system and installs it. Your design, sections and brief are untouched. A save point is made first, so undo works.
