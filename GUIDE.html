What to type, and what happens

Keep this open while you work. Find the line, copy the phrase, paste it into the chat with Claude. When Claude asks a question with options, click one.

Starting, or coming back

  1. 1Open the Claude app → Code → open your project folder.
  2. 2Set the permission mode to Auto (the selector at the bottom of the chat).
  3. 3/helpClaude says where the project is and what to say next./startNew project — pick the option that fits: a brief, a finished Figma, or a site you like.

Claude asks you for a key or a token Nothing to type. Open the Board → Settings, paste the value next to its label, press Save. It's saved on your computer only — Claude never sees it. The preview restarts by itself.

 Building and screenshots take up to a minute. After three minutes of silence, ask.
