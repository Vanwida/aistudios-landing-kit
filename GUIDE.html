How to work with the kit

You work in a chat with Claude. You say what you want, in plain words, and Claude does the building. When it needs a decision, it asks you with clickable options — click one. To see the result, look at the preview (the page) and the board (your project). If you're ever unsure what to do, type /help.

Every time you sit down

  1. 1

    Open the Claude app → Code → open your project folder.

  2. 2

    Set the permission mode to Auto — the selector at the bottom of the chat.

  3. 3

    Type /help — Claude says where the project is and what to say next. New project? Type /start instead and choose New landing page from a brief.

The work, step by step

Tap the step you're on. The next one lights up, so you always know what comes next.

If something happens

Describe what you see, in your own words. You never have to work out why.

The preview is blank or won't load
Tell Claudethe preview is blank

Claude checks the preview and restarts it.

An error appears in the chat
Tell Claudesomething's wrong

You don't need to read the error. If Claude asks what you were doing, one line is enough.

It doesn't look like my Figma
Tell Claude what's differentthe heading breaks into three lines

Say it the way you'd tell a junior. Claude compares against your frame and fixes it.

It changed something I didn't ask for
Tell Claudeyou changed the footer — put it back

More than one thing? Say undo: back to the last save point. Nothing is ever lost.

It asks a technical question
Tell ClaudeI don't know — choose the standard option and tell me in one line

Always allowed. You never have to understand the question.

It asks me to log in somewhere
Do thislog in with your own account, then tell Claude: done

Normal the first time — GitHub, Vercel, Figma. A browser window opens; log in; come back.

It's been silent for a while
Tell Claudestatus?

Building and screenshots take up to a minute. After three minutes of silence, ask.

I'm lost, or out of patience
Tell Claudesave everything and tell me where we are

Then take a break. When you come back, type /help and carry on from there.
